About

Design as a
Way of Living

Marlin Studio is a San Francisco Bay Area interior design practice founded by Qinglin Jiang. We specialize in high-end residential projects — from full-home renovations to curated furniture and material selections.

Our approach balances architectural integrity with personal warmth. Every project begins with listening: understanding how you move through space, what you collect, and what calms you. From there, we build environments that are both refined and deeply livable.

We work with an exclusive network of trade vendors and artisan makers to source pieces unavailable through retail channels, ensuring each home is genuinely one of a kind.

Process

How We Work

Step 01

Discovery

We begin with a thorough conversation about how you live, what you love, and what the space needs to accomplish. This session sets the foundation for everything that follows.

Step 02

Concept

We develop a cohesive design direction — mood boards, space plans, material palettes — and present it for your feedback before any procurement begins.

Step 03

Procurement

Once the concept is approved, we source and order all specified items, manage vendor relationships, and track delivery timelines on your behalf.

Step 04

Installation

We coordinate delivery, placement, and final styling — arriving on-site to ensure every element lands exactly as designed, down to the last detail.
